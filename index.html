    Jiemin Fang (方杰民)¶

    I am a senior researcher and assistant scientist/chief expert at Huawei, leading the Xiaoyi3D (小艺3D) team to build foundation models for 3D generation/reconstruction and physically interactive worlds. I was also selected for Huawei's TopMinds Program. My broader research interests include neural rendering, representation learning, neural architecture search, and AutoML.

    I received my PhD and BE degrees from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2023 and 2018, respectively, advised by Prof. Wenyu Liu and Prof. Xinggang Wang.

    We are hiring! Our team brings together highly creative minds. If you are interested in intern or full-time positions, please feel free to contact me via email — let's explore the world together through frontier research.
